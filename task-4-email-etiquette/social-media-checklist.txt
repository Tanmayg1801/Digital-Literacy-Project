SOCIAL MEDIA RESPONSIBILITY CHECKLIST

The DOs:

* DO often check your privacy settings. Platform conditions are subject to frequent changes, make sure that accounts you think are "private" actually are.

* DO create a polished "anchor" profile. Keep your LinkedIn profile up to date with your academic accomplishments and a professional headshot.

* Do network on purpose. To stay up to date on professional chances, follow academics, business executives, and campus organisations.

* DO show off your work. Share projects, volunteer work or new abilities you've learned in your classes using your platforms.

* DO check information before disseminating it. Making sure you are not a source of false information is the first step in safeguarding your online reputation.

The DON'Ts:

* DON'T post illegal or unprofessional content. Avoid sharing photos of excessive partying or using derogatory language.

* DON'T vent about your university or workplace. Complaining about professors or employers online suggests a lack of professional discretion.

* DON'T engage in online "flame wars." Avoid aggressive public arguments, as high-conflict digital footprints can damage your reputation.

* DON'T share your real-time location. Posting your exact campus schedule or current whereabouts creates safety risks.

* DON'T overshare personal details. Maintain a clear boundary between your private life and the public persona.
