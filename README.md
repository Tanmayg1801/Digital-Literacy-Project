## Project Overview
This repository contains my Digital Literacy Portfolio, completed as part of the CSE0001 course. Assuming the role of a Student Digital Ambassador, I have curated resources and completed exercises spanning visual communication, professional digital branding, cloud collaboration, online etiquette, and cybersecurity awareness. 

## Module Summaries & Repository Structure

### 📁 `task-1-presentation/` | Task 1: Digital Literacy Awareness
Contains an infographic designed to educate peers on safe internet practices, essential digital tools, and maintaining a professional online presence.

### 📁 `task-2-portfolio/` | Task 2: Build Your Student Digital Portfolio
Contains screenshots documenting the setup of my professional developer presence across three key platforms:
* **GitHub:** Primary codebase hosting and developer profile.
* **LinkedIn:** Academic and professional networking.
* **ResearchGate:** Reading research papers and be up-to-date with new ideas.

### 📁 `task-3-platforms/` | Task 3: Explore Coding & Collaboration Platforms
